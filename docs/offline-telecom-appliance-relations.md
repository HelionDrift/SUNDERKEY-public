# Supplied Network-Appliance Management Relations

The GC-D13-S06 partial component compares opaque, synthetic management-zone
relationships with a supplied allowlist. It records declared edges outside
that policy. Its checked example is
[`examples/offline-telecom-appliance-component.json`](../examples/offline-telecom-appliance-component.json).

All zones, services, relationships, and completeness claims are assertions.
No appliance or network is contacted. A mismatch is not proof of reachability,
compromise, or impact. Separate original-model replay is required. This is
nonqualifying R0 evidence and grants no authority.
