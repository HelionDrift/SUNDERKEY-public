# Supplied Scenario Control Triad Common Task

The checked common task combines GA-D04-S01 scenario dependency review,
GA-D04-S02 initial-access control coverage, and GA-D04-S07
privilege-transition review on one synthetic exercise record. The
three jobs retain their original requests and distinct specialist
profiles under a shared domain commander. R0, empty resources, no
credentials, and separate original-task replay are required.

This collaboration does not produce an executable chain. Its controls
are caller assertions, not verified consent or action authority.
Nothing is delivered to a target, escalated, published as a finding,
or qualified for live use.
