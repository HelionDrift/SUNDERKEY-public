# Supplied Signaling Routes

The GC-D13-S12 partial component compares opaque synthetic signaling-route
relationships with a supplied route policy. Its checked example is
[`examples/offline-signaling-routes-component.json`](../examples/offline-signaling-routes-component.json).

No SS7 or Diameter packet is formed or sent. No signaling system, subscriber,
or network is contacted. Supplied routes and completeness are assertions; a
mismatch does not demonstrate an actual interconnect or impact. The result
is nonqualifying R0 evidence and grants no authority.
