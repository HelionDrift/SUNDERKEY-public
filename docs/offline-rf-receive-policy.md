# Supplied RF Receive-Policy Projection

The GC-D13-S10 partial component compares synthetic, opaque receive-capture
labels with supplied approved-band labels. It records non-receive-only
declarations as unknown, not as authorization. Its checked example is
[`examples/offline-rf-receive-component.json`](../examples/offline-rf-receive-component.json).

No frequency, signal, subscriber data, or transmit parameter is accepted.
No radio is connected, no signal is observed, and no transmission occurs.
The policy and authorization references are assertions, not validated legal
permission. This is nonqualifying R0 evidence and grants no authority.
