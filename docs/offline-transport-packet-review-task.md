# Supplied Transport Packet Review Common Task

The checked common task combines GD-D25-S03 synthetic vehicle service-zone
declarations, GD-D25-S10 synthetic MAVLink 2 packet-envelope geometry, and
GD-D25-S07 synthetic AIS position-report structure, and GD-D25-S04 synthetic
OCPP 1.6 DataTransfer structure as distinct specialist jobs. These unrelated
fixtures are not evidence of one vehicle, aircraft, vessel, charging station,
site, or shared communication channel. The task preserves all
original requests and commander ownership, requires R0 and empty
resources, and verifies the report against the separate original task.

The collaboration does not authenticate a source, verify a MAVLink checksum
or signature, verify an AIS position, authenticate an OCPP vendor, contact a
target, publish a finding, or grant action authority or qualification.
