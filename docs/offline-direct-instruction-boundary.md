# Supplied Direct Instruction Boundary

The GD-D20-S09 partial component compares synthetic override and outcome
declarations with supplied higher-priority constraint IDs. Its checked example
is [`examples/offline-direct-instruction-boundary-component.json`](../examples/offline-direct-instruction-boundary-component.json).

No prompt or response text is accepted, no model is called, and no jailbreak
is verified. Outcomes and completeness are assertions, not observed behavior.
A declared conflict is not a finding or qualification. This is nonqualifying
R0 evidence and grants no authority.
