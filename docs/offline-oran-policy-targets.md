# Supplied Open RAN Policy Targets

The GC-D13-S19 partial component compares synthetic RIC policy-type support
declarations with supplied policy-target intents. Its checked example is
[`examples/offline-oran-policy-targets-component.json`](../examples/offline-oran-policy-targets-component.json).

No A1 request is sent, no RIC or network is contacted, and no policy is
applied. Support and intent declarations are unverified assertions. A mismatch
does not prove deployment or exploitation. The result is nonqualifying R0
evidence and grants no authority.
